### Your esports data team, at a fraction of the cost.

ScuttlerLabs turns professional esports matches into decision-grade data. We analyze every match from the broadcast video, one game at a time, and deliver the datasets and tools that coaches, analysts, and front offices use for opponent preparation, draft planning, off-season roster decisions, and scouting.

We currently cover **League of Legends** and **FC Online**, and we build custom metrics on request.

---

## Why we exist

Public stats sites show what an API hands them: win rates, KDA, pick rates. That is enough for fans, but not for a coaching staff that has to decide what to ban on Saturday or whom to sign in the off-season.

We take a different approach:

| | Typical stats services | ScuttlerLabs |
|---|---|---|
| **Source** | Aggregates from public APIs | Observed events and tags read from pro match VODs, game by game |
| **Unit of analysis** | Outcome stats (win rate, KDA, pick rate) | Process metrics that separate *choice* from *execution*, compared under matched conditions (patch, roster, opponent, side) |
| **Purpose** | Browsing | Opponent prep, draft decisions, roster and scouting calls |
| **Flexibility** | Fixed dashboards | Custom metrics built around each team's questions |

## How we work

- **Video first.** An automated pipeline reads the minimap, HUD, and scoreboard of every broadcast, cross-checks multiple models against deterministic on-screen evidence, and records what actually happened, not just the final box score.
- **Every number carries its context.** Each value ships with its denominator, date range, patch, comparison group, source matches, and uncertainty. You get the number *and* the reasons to trust it, along with its limits.
- **Data, not opinions.** We don't write narrative verdicts or recommendations. The insight lives in the metric design: tables that split choice from execution, like-for-like comparisons, and values linked back to the games behind them. The decisions stay with your staff.
- **Built to be audited.** Formulas, denominators, and validation criteria are documented, and every summary links back to the matches it came from.

## Products

### ScuttlerLabs LoL · [lol.scuttlerlabs.com](https://lol.scuttlerlabs.com/en)

Professional League of Legends analysis across **LCK, LPL, LEC, LCS, and CBLOL**.

- **Player and team ratings** adjusted for role, patch, and opponent, shown with uncertainty ranges and linked to the evidence games
- **Opponent preparation**: draft tendencies, game-plan patterns, and matched-condition comparisons for your next opponent
- **Draft tools**: pick and ban scenarios and draft simulations grounded in each team's and player's history
- **Match review**: lane-by-lane timelines, vision control, and decision-level events such as roams, ganks, and objective setups
- **Roster and scouting**: off-season planning, player comparison, and prospect evaluation for front offices and agencies
- **Team workspace**: a private space for your staff's opponent prep and scrim review

### ScuttlerLabs FC Online · [fconline.scuttlerlabs.com](https://fconline.scuttlerlabs.com/en)

Professional FC Online analysis for competitions such as **FSL, FTB, and FFL**.

- **Per-match maps** of passes, defensive actions, and shots
- **Player profiles, comparison, and similarity search**, focused on the esports player who controls the in-game team
- **Team comparison and trends over time**, with club totals attributed to the player's affiliation at the time of each match
